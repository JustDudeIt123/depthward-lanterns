# Assets

Project icon: custom geometric artwork built by tools/prepare_curseforge_assets.py.
As of 14 November 2026, lantern cuboid geometry and 16px materials are built by `tools/build_lantern_art.py` from the user's visual reference: dark metal, thin brass trim and amber panels. The oil flask is generated with built-in ImageGen and exported with nearest-neighbour sampling to exactly 16x16 RGBA. The image prompt asks for a square glass oil flask with a cork, golden oil, transparent background and a vanilla Minecraft pixel palette. The generated master remains in the user's Codex generated_images directory; the final sprite is included here.

The darkness whisper is the 19.0–24.0 second excerpt of the user-provided MP3 “Звук где призрак шепчется в темноте сам с собой.mp3” (embedded metadata: Bruni AFX / bruniafx.ru). It is mono Vorbis, 44.1 kHz, exactly five seconds, normalized toward -20 LUFS, with 0.18s/0.4s fades and a sounds.json volume of 0.75. This is a supplied recording, not synthesized audio; rights to the source recording remain with its owner. No claim of authorship of the recording is made.

Effect icons, infernal lens and project icon retain their previous provenance. The item/generated parent is a Minecraft engine template; no Minecraft texture is bundled. The older release artwork generator preserves the curated lantern/flask/whisper assets.
