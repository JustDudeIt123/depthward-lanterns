DepthWard Oil Lanterns 1.3.3
Changes since 1.2.3:

- Moved dynamic lighting integration into the lantern mod itself. DepthWard Mod Compat is no longer required for dynamic lighting.
- Added a body-facing bright 150-degree sector with smooth falloff to weaker light around the remaining 210 degrees. Placed lanterns keep their radial lighting.
- Excluded the owner's carried lantern from lighting their own character model to address movement-related flickering.
- Updated moving light sources every client tick and added per-section filtering for small accumulated light changes.
- Updated and simplified the lantern model, refreshed the oil bottle texture, and replaced the darkness whisper sound.
- Fixed the item colour callback issue that could crash item rendering in an intermediate build.

Dependencies: Curios is required. RyoamicLights 0.2.3+mc1.20.1 and its dependencies are required on the client.
Light range remains configurable. The 16-block range used by the DepthWard modpack is a pack setting, not the standalone default.
